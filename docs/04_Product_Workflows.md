# Product Workflows

This document describes the end-to-end behaviour of the Customer Intelligence Platform from the moment data is generated until users make business decisions.

The workflows focus on how the product behaves from a business perspective. Implementation details such as data stores, AI architecture, retrieval mechanisms, tool orchestration, and system design are intentionally excluded and are covered in the architecture documents.

---

# 1. Merchant and Shopper Activities

The platform continuously receives data generated by merchants and shoppers.

## Merchant Activities

Merchants interact with our SaaS platform by:

* Installing or uninstalling applications
* Starting, renewing, upgrading, downgrading, or cancelling subscriptions
* Enabling or disabling platform features
* Updating store configuration
* Creating and managing campaigns
* Using platform dashboards

These activities generate merchant events that help the platform understand merchant lifecycle, platform adoption, and business health.

---

## Shopper Activities

Shoppers interact with merchant storefronts by:

* Viewing products
* Searching products
* Adding items to Wishlist
* Using Save for Later
* Adding products to cart
* Checking out
* Completing purchases
* Interacting with campaigns
* Using merchant-enabled platform features

These activities generate shopper events that capture customer behaviour throughout the shopping journey.

---

# 2. Data Collection and Processing

The platform continuously collects merchant and shopper events from upstream systems.

Before the data becomes available for analytics, it is:

* Validated
* Checked for quality
* Standardized
* Enriched where necessary
* Organized into appropriate business datasets

The platform maintains a consistent and reliable view of merchant, shopper, campaign, and business information.

---

# 3. Business Views are Updated

As new information becomes available, the platform updates its business views.

Examples include:

* Merchant profiles
* Shopper profiles
* Customer journeys
* Business metrics
* Revenue metrics
* Conversion metrics
* Feature usage metrics
* Campaign metrics

These business views allow users to understand the current state of the platform and identify meaningful trends.

---

# 4. Users Monitor Business Performance

Users interact with dashboards appropriate to their role.

## Internal Teams

Internal teams can monitor information such as:

* Merchant health
* Merchant subscriptions
* Platform adoption
* Shopper behaviour
* Revenue trends
* Conversion trends
* Feature adoption
* Campaign performance
* Business growth

---

## Merchants

Merchants can monitor information such as:

* Store performance
* Shopper journeys
* Wishlist activity
* Save for Later activity
* Add to Cart activity
* Conversion funnels
* Revenue attribution
* Campaign performance
* Feature usage

The dashboards help users identify changes, trends, and opportunities that may require further investigation.

---

# 5. A Business Question is Raised

After reviewing dashboards or business metrics, a user may decide to investigate further.

Example questions include:

* Why did conversion decrease yesterday?
* Why is Merchant ABC at risk of churning?
* Which shopper segments respond best to campaigns?
* How can I improve my store's conversion rate?
* Why did Wishlist usage decrease last week?

The user submits the question through the platform's AI interface.

---

# 6. The Platform Investigates

The platform investigates the business question by gathering relevant information and supporting evidence.

During the investigation, the platform may review:

* Business metrics
* Merchant information
* Shopper behaviour
* Customer journeys
* Campaign performance
* Product usage
* Historical business knowledge
* Similar business situations

The investigation continues until sufficient evidence is available to explain the observed behaviour.

If sufficient information is not available, the platform clearly communicates that it cannot confidently answer the question instead of making assumptions.

---

# 7. The Platform Explains the Findings

After completing the investigation, the platform presents:

* A clear explanation of the findings
* Supporting evidence
* Relevant business observations
* Possible causes, when they can be determined
* Recommendations, when actionable opportunities are identified

The objective is to help users understand what happened and support better business decisions.

---

# 8. The User Makes a Decision

Based on the investigation, users can make informed decisions.

Examples include:

### Product Team

* Prioritize new product features
* Improve existing functionality
* Investigate further if required

### Marketing Team

* Create new campaign strategies
* Recommend campaigns to merchants
* Identify new shopper segments

### Customer Success

* Contact merchants at risk of churn
* Recommend feature adoption
* Prepare customer success discussions

### Support Team

* Resolve merchant issues
* Explain platform behaviour
* Guide merchants through troubleshooting

### Merchants

* Improve store configuration
* Launch campaigns
* Enable additional platform features
* Improve shopper engagement
* Increase conversion and revenue

---

# 9. Continuous Improvement

As merchants and shoppers continue interacting with the platform, new data is continuously generated.

This allows the platform to:

* Refresh business metrics
* Improve customer intelligence
* Support future investigations
* Continuously provide up-to-date insights for business users

---

# Workflow Summary

The overall product workflow can be summarized as follows.

```text
Merchant and shopper activities
                │
                ▼
Data is collected and processed
                │
                ▼
Business views and metrics are updated
                │
                ▼
Users monitor dashboards
                │
                ▼
A business question is raised
                │
                ▼
The platform investigates
                │
                ▼
The platform explains the findings
                │
                ▼
The user makes an informed business decision
                │
                ▼
New activity generates additional data
                │
                ▼
The cycle continues
```

The Customer Intelligence Platform is designed to transform continuous merchant and shopper activity into meaningful business insights that help internal teams and merchants make faster, evidence-based decisions.
